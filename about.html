ABOUT UPGRADE BARBERSHOP

Upgrade Barbershop is a Cape Town barbershop based on the idea of creating a transformation through grooming, aesthetics and personal style.

Our goal is to help clients find a look that suits their appearance and makes them feel confident.

See our services and prices or book an appointment.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

VISAGISM • GROOMING • AESTHETICS

Visagism means choosing a haircut and grooming style that works with the shape of your face and your natural features. Every Upgrade appointment starts with understanding what suits you. Have a question first? Send us an enquiry.
